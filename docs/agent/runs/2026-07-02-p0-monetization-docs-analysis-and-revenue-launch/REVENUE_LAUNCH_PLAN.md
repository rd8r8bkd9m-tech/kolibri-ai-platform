# Go-To-Market Plan 2026-07-02

## 24 Hours

Goal: turn existing assets into a controlled selling surface without spending money or sending mass outreach.

1. Freeze the public offer menu:
   - Telegram AI bot for leads/FAQ.
   - AI documents/КП/estimates assistant.
   - Bot audit/repair.
   - API/webhook integration.
   - Mini App/PWA prototype.
2. Prepare the second Kwork offer draft:
   - recommended: `Создам AI-бота для документов, КП, отчетов или смет`;
   - starter: 10 000-20 000 RUB, 3-5 days;
   - proof: estimate editor, PDF/document generation, safe synthetic screenshots.
3. Prepare landing page copy:
   - headline: "Telegram AI bots and document automation for service businesses";
   - sections: offers, proof, process, safety, examples, intake form.
4. Prepare a repo-local CRM seed:
   - lead id, source, ICP, pain, offer, expected price, status, next action.
5. Convert the existing Kwork Wave A queue into 5 personalized proposal drafts.
6. Owner approval gate before sending any new public proposals.

## 72 Hours

1. Publish or stage two more fixed-scope offers after live competitor re-check:

2. Build or draft a simple public landing/readme page:
   - no payments;

5. Start daily revenue report:

## Two Weeks

Goal: convert marketplace activity into repeatable services and prepare B2B pilot packaging.

Week 1:

- Keep 3-5 narrow offers active.
- Send only owner-approved or already-delegated safe Kwork proposals.
- Turn replies into sanitized intake rows.
- Deliver any first small audit/bot/doc task through the factory with clear artifacts.
- Add one public-safe portfolio case per completed demo/order.

Week 2:

- Double down on offers with replies.
- Raise price only after first positive delivery/review evidence.
- Create B2B pilot deck for "AI document/estimate automation for small contractors".
- Package the Control Center/Agent Factory as a private operations capability, not a public self-serve SaaS.
- Decide whether to build Stripe/Kwork-only/manual invoice flow later; do not implement payment flows now.

## Sales Motions

### Kwork Motion

- Use existing safe-chat-only autonomy policy.
- Start with prepared Wave A projects.
- Keep proposals specific: problem, 3-stage plan, price, deadline, questions.
- No external contacts, no off-platform payments, no secret requests in chat.

### B2B Manual Motion

- Build a list manually.
- Ask owner approval before first external batch.
- Send no mass campaign.
- Offer a paid audit or prototype, not a platform subscription.

### Landing Motion

- Landing is a trust surface and manual intake.
- CTA: "Send task description" or "Request fixed-scope estimate".
- No payment widget until payments policy is approved.

## Metrics

Daily:

- proposals drafted;
- proposals sent;
- replies;
- qualified leads;
- orders;
- revenue booked;
- delivery tasks opened;
- blockers.

Weekly:

- offer views;
- reply rate;
- conversion by offer;
- average ticket;
- delivery time;
- disputes/risk events;
- reusable assets created.

## No-Go List

- Broad "we build any AI/SaaS" offer.
- Guaranteed sales or rankings.
- Mass spam.
- Client secrets in GitHub.
- Payment/subscription work without policy.
- External Kwork contact/payment bypass.
- Large custom project without discovery and staged scope.
