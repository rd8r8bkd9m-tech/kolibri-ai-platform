# Kwork 14-Day Revenue Sprint

## Goal

Start earning on Kwork by reducing buyer risk, adding narrow offers, improving trust signals and sending careful owner-approved responses. Do not chase "big AI system" orders first; build a small reliable funnel.

## Current Bottleneck

The profile looks high-skill but high-risk for a new buyer:

- 1 active kwork;
- high competition;
- high starting price;
- 0 sales;
- 0 positive reviews;
- 1 negative review;
- broad positioning.

## Revenue Strategy

1. Add 3-5 narrow services first.
2. Use lower entry packages for first trust-building orders.
3. Keep every offer fixed-scope.
4. Use buyer requirements to avoid disputes.
5. Reply manually and professionally.
6. Convert portfolio from screenshots into business outcomes.

## First 3 Offers To Publish

### 1. Telegram bot with AI functions

Recommended entry price: 15 000-25 000 RUB.

Why first: visible demand in current Kwork project feed, understandable deliverable, matches Vladislav's skills.

### 2. AI automation for one routine business process

Recommended entry price: 7 000-15 000 RUB.

Why second: good fit for documents, estimates, tables, reports and applications; lower scope than a full app.

### 3. Site/bot/API/code audit with report

Recommended entry price: 5 000-10 000 RUB.

Why third: easiest first sale and review path; low delivery risk; produces portfolio proof.

## Daily 60-Minute Routine

1. Check Kwork messages and orders.
2. Review new buyer projects.
3. Draft 5-10 personalized responses; send only owner-approved text.
4. Improve one offer element: title, cover, FAQ, requirements or package.
5. Improve one portfolio case.
6. Complete one Kwork learning block until 8/8.
7. Record views/messages/orders in daily report.

## Do Not Do

- Do not scale the expensive broad SPA/PWA offer as the only offer.
- Do not create 9 nearly identical kworks for the platform checklist.
- Do not promise guaranteed revenue or "any AI system".
- Do not accept unclear urgent projects without requirements.
- Do not move payments or communication outside Kwork if it violates rules.
