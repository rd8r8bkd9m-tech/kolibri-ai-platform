# Kwork Risk And Rules

## Core Rules

- No spam.
- No fake reviews.
- No fake portfolio.
- No fake credentials.
- No platform manipulation.
- No external payment bypass.
- No unrealistic promises.
- No hidden subscriptions.
- No client data leakage.
- No use of confidential client data in public portfolio.
- No financial action without owner approval.

## Kwork-Specific Safety Gates

Before publishing a profile change or kwork:

- confirm exact title and description with owner;
- verify claims are true;
- verify price/scope/delivery time are realistic;
- confirm no forbidden data is included;
- confirm no payment/security/tax settings are touched.

Before sending a client message:

- owner approves the exact text;
- scope and deadline are realistic;
- no outside-payment or platform-bypass request is included;
- no private credentials are requested in chat.

Before accepting an order:

- owner approves;
- requirements are clear;
- deadline is realistic;
- technical dependencies are available;
- risk of dispute is acceptable.

## Main Profile Risks

- One public negative review and zero positive reviews.
- Current main kwork is expensive and in very high competition.
- Existing skills/tags are partly too technical for buyers.
- Too few active kworks for marketplace discovery.
- Some service categories may require careful scope to avoid underpricing or disputes.

## Risk Controls

- Start with small fixed-scope work.
- Use buyer requirements aggressively.
- Avoid urgent/unclear projects without clear inputs.
- Keep all promises measurable.
- Keep revisions limited.
- Use synthetic/demo portfolio data when client data is private.
- Track every sent message and accepted order.
