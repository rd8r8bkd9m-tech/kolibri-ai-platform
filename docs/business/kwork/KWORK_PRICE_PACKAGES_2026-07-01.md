## Pricing Rules

- Keep payment and communication inside Kwork.
- Discuss price, budget, scope, stages, deadlines and add-ons inside Kwork.
- Do not offer guaranteed revenue, guaranteed moderation, fake engagement, bypasses or off-platform support.
- Do not include hosting, paid APIs, domains, paid accounts or external subscriptions unless the client provides them through safe Kwork-approved handling.
- Start with a narrow deliverable, then offer a second paid stage.
