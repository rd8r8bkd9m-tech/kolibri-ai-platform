## Why This Setting Matters

According to Kwork public materials, confirmed self-employed status can:

- make the seller visible for company orders paid by bank transfer;
- reduce withdrawal commission for orders from companies;
- reduce withdrawal commission for orders from individuals compared with the default rate;
- add a public trust signal that the seller works with companies.

For orders from companies, Kwork guidance may require the seller to form a receipt/check and attach the check link to the relevant order. Tax and accounting decisions stay with the owner and official tax/accounting sources.
