## One-Page Sales Narrative

Kolibri helps service businesses turn customer requests into structured intake, estimates, documents, and follow-up workflows using Telegram bots, AI document assistants, API integrations, and focused Mini App/PWA prototypes.

The first paid motion should be a small, fixed-scope pilot. The buyer should understand exactly what they get, what is excluded, what needs human approval, and what is measured.

## Target Buyers

- Owner-operated service businesses with repeated quote, estimate, or document work.
- Teams coordinating through Telegram or willing to use Telegram as a lightweight operations channel.
- Contractors, renovation, engineering, fit-out, clinics, education, agencies, consultants, and local service companies.
- Teams with existing bots, forms, spreadsheets, or CRMs that are not connected well.

Not a fit:

- fully autonomous sales decisions without review;
- regulated legal, medical, financial, tax, or insurance advice without qualified review;
- enterprise procurement as the first motion;
- spam, scraping, fake engagement, or platform-rule bypass.

### 2. Revenue Leak

Most teams do not lose revenue because demand is absent. They lose it because intake, estimating, quoting, and follow-up are too manual.

Proof points to discuss:

- customer requests arrive in several channels;
- data is copied by hand;
- estimates and КП are delayed;
- follow-up is missed;
- owners lack a daily view of open opportunities.

### 3. What Kolibri Builds

- Telegram lead/FAQ/support bots.
- AI estimate and document assistants.
- Bot audit and repair sprints.
- API/webhook/CRM/table integrations.
- Mini App/PWA prototypes.

Core control:

```text
AI drafts and organizes. Humans approve business-critical outputs.
```

### 4. Use Case: AI Estimates

Workflow:

1. Customer request arrives through Telegram, email, form, or call notes.
2. AI extracts job type, location, scope, measurements, constraints, photos, deadlines, and missing questions.
3. Draft estimate or КП is generated from an approved template.
4. Human reviews and approves.
5. Follow-up status is tracked.

Value:

- faster first response;
- fewer missing fields;
- more consistent estimates;
- less copy-paste.
