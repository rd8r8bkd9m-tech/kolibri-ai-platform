## Positioning

Kolibri builds practical AI bots and workflow automation for service businesses that need faster intake, cleaner estimates, better follow-up, and less manual document work.

The commercial wedge is not "generic AI platform". The first offer should be a fixed-scope service with a clear buyer outcome:

- Telegram AI bot for leads, FAQ, and manager handoff.
- AI assistant for documents, КП, estimates, acts, invoices, and reports.
- Audit and repair of an existing Telegram/AI bot.
- API, webhook, CRM, or table integration around a bot/workflow.
- One-screen Telegram Mini App or PWA prototype.

## Problem

Most service businesses lose time and deals in the handoff:

- requests arrive across chats, calls, emails, forms, and files;
- important details are copied manually into estimates, КП, invoices, acts, spreadsheets, or CRMs;
- Telegram bots fail when users answer unexpectedly;
- follow-ups are easy to miss;
- owners cannot see which lead, estimate, or customer reply is blocked.

Kolibri focuses on one repeatable workflow first, ships a controlled first version, and keeps human approval where business risk matters.

## Offer Blocks

### Telegram AI Bot For Leads And FAQ

One-line:

```text
Telegram bot that captures leads, answers common questions, and hands off structured requests to a manager.
```

Best for:

- local and professional services;
- repair, installation, clinic, education, consulting, and agency teams;
- businesses where Telegram already handles customer requests.

Starter deliverables:

- one core intake scenario;
- 3-5 commands, buttons, or question steps;
- lead summary and manager handoff;
- FAQ or scripted response block;
- test checklist and handoff notes.

### AI Documents, КП, Estimates And Reports

One-line:

```text
AI-assisted workflow that turns messy inputs into reviewed estimates, commercial offers, reports, invoices, or acts.
```

Best for:

- contractors, renovation, engineering, fit-out, field service, and B2B services;
- teams preparing repeated КП, estimates, invoices, reports, or post-work documents;
- owners who want faster drafts without losing final review.

Starter deliverables:

- one document type;
- structured input template;
- draft generation flow;
- deterministic checks for numeric fields where applicable;
- human review checklist.

### Bot Audit And Repair

One-line:

```text
Focused review and repair plan for Telegram, AI, webhook, or automation flows that are unreliable or unfinished.
```

Best for:

- existing bots that users abandon;
- broken webhooks or silent failures;
- inconsistent AI prompts;
- projects where the original builder is unavailable.

Starter deliverables:

- failure-point review;
- risk-ranked findings;
- implementation sequence;
- small fix if included in scope;
- maintenance handoff notes.

### API, Webhook, CRM Or Table Integration

## Process

1. Diagnose the workflow, customer path, current tools, and revenue goal.
2. Scope fixed deliverables, inputs, exclusions, assumptions, and acceptance criteria.
3. Build the smallest useful workflow.
4. Test realistic examples, edge cases, and failure paths.
5. Hand off usage notes, limitations, and next recommended improvements.

## FAQ

Can Kolibri build a Telegram bot from scratch?

Yes, if the scope defines the scenario, commands, data collection, handoff, AI role, and acceptance checklist.

Can Kolibri fix an existing bot?

Yes. The safest first step is an audit or repair sprint with source/log access sanitized for secrets.

Can AI send estimates automatically?

For business-critical estimates, AI should draft and structure the work. A human should approve before customer-facing commitments are sent.

Are paid APIs, hosting, domains, or subscriptions included?

No. They are excluded unless separately approved and scoped.

Will Kolibri publish pages or message prospects?

No. Publication, outbound messages, paid actions, account mutation, and contract/payment actions require separate owner approval.
