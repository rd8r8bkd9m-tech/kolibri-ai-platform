# Sales Materials Seed 2026-07-02

## Core Positioning

Kolibri builds practical AI bots and document automation for small teams: Telegram bots, AI document/estimate workflows, API integrations, Mini Apps and bot audits. First delivery is fixed-scope, testable and safe; larger systems are split into stages.

## Offer 1: Telegram AI Bot For Leads

One-line:

```text
Сделаю Telegram AI-бота для заявок, FAQ и передачи клиента менеджеру.
```

Discovery questions:

- What should the bot collect from a client?
- Which questions repeat most often?
- Where should a lead be sent: chat, table, CRM, webhook?
- Is AI needed in the first version or is a scripted FAQ enough?
- What is the acceptance checklist?

Starter boundary:

- one scenario;
- up to 3-5 commands/buttons;
- simple lead capture;
- test checklist;
- no complex CRM or paid API included.

## Offer 2: AI Bot For Documents, КП, Estimates And Reports

One-line:

```text
Соберу AI-сценарий или прототип для смет, КП, актов, счетов и отчетов.
```

Discovery questions:

- Which document type is first?
- What input is available?
- What output must be generated?
- Which numeric fields require deterministic checks?
- Is PDF/CSV/export needed?

Starter boundary:

- one document type;
- synthetic or redacted examples;
- structured output template;
- human review required;
- no legal/accounting guarantee.

## Offer 3: Bot Audit And Repair

One-line:

```text
Проверю Telegram/AI-бота, найду ошибки и дам план безопасной доработки.
```

Discovery questions:

- What is broken or underperforming?
- Which framework/platform is used?
- Is code access available?
- Are there logs with secrets removed?
- What should be fixed first?

Starter boundary:

- report only or small fix;
- no full rewrite in audit scope;
- no secret exposure in chat;
- no production deploy unless separately agreed.

## Offer 4: API/Webhook/CRM/Table Integration

One-line:

```text
Интегрирую бота, форму или backend с API, webhook, CRM или таблицей.
```

Discovery questions:

- What source sends data?
- What target receives data?
- What fields are required?
- What should happen on errors?
- How will credentials be handed off safely?

Starter boundary:

- one endpoint or webhook;
- sample payload;
- test request/response;
- no complex OAuth or enterprise rollout without separate scope.

## Offer 5: Telegram Mini App / PWA Prototype

One-line:

```text
Сделаю простой Mini App/PWA экран для формы, статуса, заявки или кабинета.
```

Discovery questions:

- What is the one primary screen?
- What data does the user enter or see?
- Does it connect to an existing bot/backend?
- Is auth required?
- What devices must be checked?

Starter boundary:

- one screen or one short flow;
- basic responsive UI;
- no full marketplace/SaaS;
- backend/API integration only if selected package includes it.

```text
Здравствуйте. По задаче вижу понятный первый этап: [specific outcome].

Предлагаю сделать фиксированный MVP:
1. уточнить сценарий и входные данные;
2. собрать [bot/workflow/integration/prototype];
3. проверить на тестовых примерах и передать инструкцию.

Срок: [N] дней. Бюджет: [range/fixed]. В стоимость не включены платные API, хостинг, домены и сторонние сервисы, если они нужны.

Чтобы точно оценить, подскажите:
- [question 1]
- [question 2]
- [question 3]

Работа и переписка остаются здесь, в Kwork, чтобы все условия и защита сделки были сохранены.
```

## Red Lines For Sales Copy

- Do not guarantee revenue, conversion, moderation, ranking or perfect AI answers.
- Do not claim full legal/accounting/medical/security replacement.
- Do not ask for tokens in public chat.
- Do not offer off-platform payment or contacts.
- Do not include paid APIs, hosting, domains or subscriptions by default.
