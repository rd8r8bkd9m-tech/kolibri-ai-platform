## Purpose

Draft business and revenue tasks for Kolibri Factory. Identify monetization
opportunities, prepare service offerings, and structure client engagement plans.

## Trigger

- Owner requests business task planning
- Revenue opportunity identified
- Client engagement requires preparation
- Kwork/freelance platform tasks needed

## Inputs

- Business objectives
- Available skills and capabilities
- Market context
- Owner preferences

## Outputs

- Business task draft with deliverables
- Pricing recommendations
- Client communication templates
- Revenue tracking plan

## Safety Constraints

- No financial transactions without owner approval
- No client commitments without owner confirmation
- No deceptive marketing or false claims
- No violation of platform terms of service
- Owner approval required for all external communications

## Dependencies

- Kolibri skill registry
- Available agent capabilities
- Platform access (Kwork, etc.)
