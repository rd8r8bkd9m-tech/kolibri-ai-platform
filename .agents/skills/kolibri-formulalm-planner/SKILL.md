## Purpose

Plan and coordinate FormulaLM research tasks within Kolibri Factory. Define
research objectives, resource requirements, and success criteria.

## Trigger

- Owner requests FormulaLM research
- New FormulaLM model or technique available
- Research milestone requires planning
- Resource allocation for research work

## Inputs

- Research objective description
- Available compute resources
- Existing FormulaLM knowledge base
- Time constraints

## Outputs

- Research plan with milestones
- Resource requirements estimate
- Success criteria definition
- Risk assessment

## Safety Constraints

- No unauthorized compute resource allocation
- No financial commitments without owner approval
- Research plans require owner review before execution
- Read-only research exploration preferred

## Dependencies

- FormulaLM documentation
- Available compute inventory
- Research knowledge base
